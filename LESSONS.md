# Lessons for KOML

Supplied quality must be scoped to the task and observation method. Routing cannot establish future quality, even when its threshold arithmetic is exact. The build validated deterministic cheapest-eligible selection against 200 planted cases, including exact-threshold and infeasible cases. It did not validate production model quality or buyer demand.

An assumed positive saving is unsafe: a baseline can fail quality while costing less than the eligible selection. The tests preserve a negative delta instead of presenting every routing change as savings. Infeasible cases must also be excluded from baseline comparisons rather than counted as free completions.

Operating rule: establish explicit evidence provenance and independently hand-computed expectations before implementing aggregate metrics. Keep synthetic proof separate from real-use validation.
